# Fusion boundary cut research

Adds independently API-authored rectangular cut controls and a deterministic command line inspector under `re/Fusion/Reconstruction/BoundaryCuts`. The inspector follows native timeline and target body joins and measures typed planar journal states. It does not modify production reader qualification.

See [the research findings](../../../../re/Fusion/Reconstruction/BoundaryCuts/Findings.md) for commands and evidence. Four cuts with varied coordinates, dimensions, corner and depth matched native volume, area, topology counts and bounds. Healthy controls bracketed the capture. Native dimension edits and restoration observations are retained. Strict neutral decoding, live parametric conversion and vendor round trip acceptance remain unfinished. No local test suite was run.
