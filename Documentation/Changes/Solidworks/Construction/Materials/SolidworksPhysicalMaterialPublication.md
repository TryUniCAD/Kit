# Publish measured physical material assignments

The registry, adapter and direct SOLIDWORKS writer publish two measured
origin-anchored rectangle bosses with a named physical material. The
single result body owns the neutral material reference. The configured
catalog, material name and SI density reach the actual part and active
configuration owners. The shared composer generates the corner origin
coincidence or the centered diagonal geometry and its owned relations.

Approval uses the exact digest of the generated archive and the pinned
SOLIDWORKS 2026 SP05.2 lifecycle report. The two cases previously passed
controlled native rebuilds, edits, saves and fresh reopens. Two guarded
SDK processes regenerated both complete archives from the neutral model
and its JSON round trip. All three SDK routes agreed and refused changed
material, missing ownership, unsupported material scope, altered evidence
and a different epoch before writing. [Authority](../../../../../re/solidworks/measurements/2026/AssignedMaterialPhysicalAuthority01.json) · [Guarded SDK replay](../../../../../re/solidworks/measurements/2026/SdkAssignedMaterialPhysical01.json)
