# Native save observation for baked FreeCAD box tolerances

The research packet embeds the verified tolerances of two disclosed
standalone FreeCAD boxes in complete generated SOLIDWORKS 2026 parts.
Two deterministic host processes produce the same containers. The serial
oracle protocol measures each part before and after a rebuild, saves
a separate native copy and reopens it read-only. An exact saved audit
checks whether the 26 typed tolerance fields survive. Native results
and intact-source acceptance remain pending.
