# Read the actual stock of a saved revolve cut

The default SOLIDWORKS part reader projects two independent full annular Front-plane bosses and one partial revolve cut with an explicit selected stock. It reads the three live D1 angle parameters, selected sketch axes and actual cut dependency. The two independently owned saved solids retain their source body names and typed face membership.

Both disclosed sources and their edited derivatives passed strict readback and eight linked stream replays. Additional private writer or unrestricted scope support requires its own evidence.
