# Cache-free ordinary revolve geometry

Open a supported version-19000 part using the normal strict reader. When the native body cache is physically absent, the SDK checks the complete feature and configuration inventory and uses the typed rectangle, selected sketch axis and live positive angle to build a closed solid. A full turn has four swept faces. A partial turn has four swept faces and two caps. Both sweep directions preserve their signed geometry.

Four generated native inputs pass eight path and byte reads, four semantic-only reads and volume and area comparisons against SOLIDWORKS. The opposite-direction pair has mirrored sweep bounds. Four native-saved controls retain their decoded geometry payloads. Five physically altered archives fail to enter the cache-free geometry route. [Readback evidence](../../../../../re/solidworks/measurements/2026/SdkColdRevolveGeometry01.json)

The measured profile uses a single ordinary Front-plane rectangle strictly to the right of its upward construction axis. Multi-feature revolutions, other end conditions, thin profiles and arbitrary contours require separate qualification.
