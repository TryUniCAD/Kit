# Initial body creation in cold SOLIDWORKS prisms

The strict reader exposes the sole boss of a fully validated cache-free
rectangular prism as a neutral `create` operation. The typed history and the
computed B-rep must agree on the one body and its producing feature. The
logical body label follows the actual boss when there is no authored output
atom. An explicit atom label retains its own provenance.

History-only reads, cache-backed parts and later joins keep their existing
operation semantics. The change adds no native export profile.
