# Read cold SOLIDWORKS two-stock ownership

The guarded research decoder reads the actual linked archive for a
cold two-stock part. It derives the selected stock from the cut's
stored wrapper and validates the stock atoms, typed D1 chains and
signed support frames. The returned objects retain their archive
identity for subsequent geometry analysis.

The proof uses the exact disclosed generated archive and an
independently generated second-selected part. It compares eight
linked stream payloads and rejects fifteen corrupt owner cases.
The decoder covers the measured provisional graph. Saved cut
results and face carriers require the separate saved-state path.

The internal geometry reader derives both solids from the reached
rectangle and circle owners. It matches the disclosed initial and
edited native geometry without a stored body cache.
