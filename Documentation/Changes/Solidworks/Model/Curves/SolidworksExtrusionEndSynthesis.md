# Shared extrusion termination and direction synthesis

The existing research extrusion model now composes reversed blind, mid-plane and two-direction blind bosses from the same profile, native feature, end specification and live dimension graphs. It uses no recorded payload, stream patch, saved shape, new per-case program or CAD runtime. The complete [2026 source lock](../../../../../re/solidworks/measurements/2026/EndConditionSourceLock02.json) precedes this writer change. Its separate discovery/control reader passed 126 complete critical archive comparisons before the new constructors were implemented.

Research requests use `feature_kind: extrusion`, `depth_m`, an explicit `end_condition` of `blind`, `mid_plane` or `two_directions`, and Boolean `reverse_direction`. Only two-direction requests supply `reverse_depth_m`. Existing `blind_extrusion` inputs retain forward single-ended behavior. All active lengths are finite and at least `1e-14` metres; unproven terminations, combinations, references and profiles fail closed. This does not enable the public adapter.

The shared end specification computes its single-end flag from the direction count, first termination code from the modeled condition and reversal field from the Boolean input. The first/second active depths follow their actual independent `D1`/`D2` parameter graphs. The constructor retains four distance/angular graphs and the transient from-offset graph with their own identities and allocator progression. Disabled constructor depths remain derived from the first depth. Active distance dimensions also generate the corresponding keyword entries; the second direction adds its own editable dimension rather than a scalar substitute.

Three new neutral process requests vary direction, end condition, plane, radial mode and independent depths through the same guarded complete-file path. The native observer requires initial body presence, geometry and constraint parity, correct direction flags, analytic mass targets, separate radial/first-depth/second-depth edits, saving and edited fresh reopening between healthy controls. Verification at introduction is source/AST and formatting inspection only. New generation, backward byte-identity, native geometry and edit/reopen results remain pending. Held-out native payloads stay sealed and every full-family/production acceptance flag remains false.

The first remote generation job passed 193 other native-format cases and 171 policy cases, but the new process loop stopped on an invalid Right-plane [dimension text point](../../../../Gotchas/Conversion/Solidworks/Profile/DimensionTextUsesWorldCoordinates.md). The Front candidate completed its guarded construction before that rejection. Corrected requests supply explicit world text points on their admitted planes; no constructor or guard was weakened. A complete new generation and native measurement remain required, and the failed batch adds no accepted new body outcomes.
