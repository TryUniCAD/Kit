# Dimensioned SOLIDWORKS assembly held-outs

The SOLIDWORKS 2026 research generator produced two independent pre-locked assembly bundles with three and four occurrences of one dimensioned rectangular part. The bundles used generated archives. Candidate and request digests were committed before the native comparison files and observations were opened.

Run the pinned prediction, binding and native observation steps in the [held-out protocol](../../../../../../re/solidworks/records/DimensionedAssemblyHeldoutProtocol2026.md). The comparison makes independent edits to width, height and depth and saves each modified child before its parent. A fresh process reopens each saved pair.

All six trials matched the independently authored feature trees, configurations, occurrence placements, solid counts, mass properties and dimension values. All 210 comparison gates passed across 26 distinct processes with two healthy controls. Native opens, rebuilds and saves reported zero errors and warnings. Scope covers one repeated rectangular child, one configuration and zero mates. Production conversion coverage remains zero.
