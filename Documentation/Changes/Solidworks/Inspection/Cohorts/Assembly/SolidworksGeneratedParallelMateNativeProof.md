# Validate a generated parallel mate in SOLIDWORKS

The frozen SOLIDWORKS 2026 trial opened and rebuilt one generated assembly with an aligned parallel Front Plane mate. Both ordered references resolved to the exact native plane objects. The assembly retained two bodies, its authored transforms, and the `0.02261 m` plane separation. Both A19 controls passed in fresh processes. All three opens reported zero errors and warnings.

Use the read-only native observer and the locked inputs described in the construction protocol. The receipt binds the measured outputs to the committed writer and records unchanged input hashes. Driving edits, angular correction, persistence and held-out coverage remain unmeasured.
