# SOLIDWORKS 2026 KeyWords corpus framing

The research audit decodes and reassembles the complete KeyWords stream of
every tracked part and assembly under the pinned 2026 research tree. The 80
parts and eight assemblies use version-4 containers and model archive
version 19000.

Compact XML reassembly reproduced all 88 streams byte for byte. The known
one-byte marker, UTF-8 declaration, two CRLF pairs and compact empty nodes
agree throughout the sample. The receipt retains container and stream hashes,
element inventories and the observed single-child references. It measures
framing and reassembly of decoded XML.
