# Record SOLIDWORKS 2026 SP05.2 archive compatibility

The [archive compatibility record](../../../re/solidworks/records/Solidworks2026ArchiveCompatibility.md)
documents the exact 2026 build, the relocated signature arrays, the
archive map handoff, and the field owners behind sampled counts. Use
this record when comparing an archive with the pinned build.

The offline comparison matched all 1,000 rows of the 2025 signature
inventory, checked 13 saved containers, and traced counts in five
archives. The [build manifest](../../../re/solidworks/v2026/Manifest2026.json)
and [measurement receipt](../../../re/solidworks/measurements/2026/ReverificationCountOwners01.json)
record module and fixture identities and the measured source revision.

The evidence covers the stated build and saved samples. Complete
record semantics and held-out native conversion acceptance remain
open in the [conversion backlog](../../Maintenance/ConversionBacklog.md).
