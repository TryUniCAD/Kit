# Write one signed angle-distance edge chamfer

The SOLIDWORKS part writer accepts a forward blind Front Plane rectangle and one live `ChamferFeature` in `distance_angle` mode. Supply the chamfer's independently driving length `D1` and angle `D2`, its preceding boss, the selected edge and the incident face that fixes the setback direction. The ordinary part writer generates the stock, ordered support references, editable dimensions and feature history from the portable model.

The public registry and direct adapter produce the ten exact streams of both previously measured signed candidates at their original locator and clock. Guarded transport checks cover binary streams and staged files. The two original source-locked designs passed native distance or angle edits, save and fresh reopen. Equal-distance and separate distance-pair modes require independent qualification. Strict SDK geometry reading of the cold part still requires a chamfer-specific cache-free projection.
