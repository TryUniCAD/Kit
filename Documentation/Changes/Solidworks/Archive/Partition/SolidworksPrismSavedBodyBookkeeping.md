# SOLIDWORKS saved prism body bookkeeping probes

The ordinary prism research composer can record its generated solid-body count and model flags for the completed rebuild. The probe exposes separate options for each field, retaining the prior generated default and permitting an independent comparison of the two changes.

The count comes from the generated solid atom owned by the active configuration. The rebuild bits follow the traced part force-rebuild and completion callbacks. Use `--record-saved-body-count` and `--record-rebuild-completion` with the existing native prism preparation tool. [Field rules and trial scope](../../../../../re/solidworks/records/PrismOpenBodyAdmission2026.md#persisted-body-bookkeeping)

Eight guarded generated variants and two pristine native controls verify both fields independently on two shapes. Every variant retains the original first-open warning and restores the correct geometry after rebuild. The two previously generated inputs reproduce with their original bytes.
