# Discovery control eligibility

SOLIDWORKS file runs fit dimension encodings, mirrored fields and pattern counts using native hashes registered for discovery in the pinned corpus or in successful paired captures for discovery recipes. A reserved or quarantined hash takes precedence over a discovery registration. Supplying a copy through `--control` preserves its classification. Plausible scalar values alone cannot enroll a control.

Inspect `Input-dimension-evidence.json` for the number of eligible controls, whether the input can take part in a changed-save experiment, and the disposition of each supplied hash. Same-family dimension matches and operand variation describe repeatable measurements within one authoring lineage. The reader leaves unqualified mirror and pattern-count tables empty.

Five local CLI runs at revision `392237d28` completed their scheduled workers without a supervisor failure. The rectangle width changed from 40 to 41 mm and the height from 20 to 23 mm. Both comparisons matched their saved reopen measurements. The cut reader matched eight dimensions. The turbine wheel retained 13 ambiguous dimension entries and three with no matching record. An explicitly supplied V8 cover was excluded from training. The block and gear provided no discovery samples for their pattern counts. All five runs reported incomplete whole-file coverage.

An extrusion at the end of the feature stream has a different boundary from one followed by another feature. The decoder now keeps those rules separate and requires every learned copy to have the expected sign. On the discovery cut, the rules located boss depth at byte 8478 and cut depth at byte 15331. Both matched the public reader spans. No such rule matched the turbine wheel.
