# Identify a research input by content

Use `kit run --root . auto --input <file> --directory <new-directory>` when the extension or authoring application is unknown. The file command recognizes the native Fusion design streams, SOLIDWORKS part keywords, SOLIDWORKS assembly component trees and standalone ASM headers. Identified design documents go through the normal file-driven decoder. Other compound containers remain unidentified.

For an ASM stream the runner attempts the existing cylinder, prism and frustum model readers and records the typed result or refusal in `Input-raw-structure.json`. Model reconstruction does not independently qualify the whole file. For unrecognized data the same report enumerates length-prefixed record chains reaching the exact input end with at least three records and more than one observed length. It searches starting offsets zero through sixteen, word widths one, two, four and eight, both meaningful endian orders, two size conventions, and at most 16,384 records per chain. A chain is a hypothesis about boundaries.

The existing native prism reader decoded the ordinary rectangle snapshot as 40 × 30 × 25 and the saved cuboid as 17 × 23 × 37 mm. Both reported their base point at the origin. The input bytes matched the exact snapshot member in a hashed archive with archived Fusion measurements. Each predicted bounding box, face/edge/vertex count and body volume matched its native observation. The measured volumes were 30 and 14.467 cm³. These measurements exercise the standalone reader path. They do not establish feature history or complete application acceptance.
