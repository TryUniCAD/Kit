# Fusion cuboid snapshot ownership research

The snapshot owner helper stages the known Design links in a fresh constrained cuboid graph. It allocates two typed topology-cache owners, binds the solid, rectangular region and sketch sheet by their emitted roles, and derives a unique snapshot member name from the generated asset and component. The three top-entity address values are an explicit research input. The helper supplies no default or archive publication path. See the [ownership notes](../../../../re/Fusion/SavedCuboidSnapshot/README.md).

The strict saved-cuboid reader requires one typed prism snapshot for the saved fixed-corner profile. It checks reciprocal history and cache serializer ownership. It joins the snapshot member name, its two topology caches and the selected sketch to the ordered passive roots. The [snapshot gotcha](../../../Gotchas/Conversion/Fusion/Geometry/Snapshots/SavedCuboidSnapshotOwner.md) explains why valid geometry alone cannot establish those joins.

## Use and limits

Call `stage_snapshot_owners` on the existing author state after starting from a fresh semantic cuboid request, including the rectangle dimensions and extrusion links. Pass distinct positive passive root IDs through `SnapshotRootPlan` and three explicit unsigned 64-bit addresses. The result is the archive member name for the matching `DocumentSnap`. Run this through the research author graph only. A complete independently generated archived owner graph still needs native qualification.

The current public production writer admission and the existing saved rectangle's read-only policy stay in effect. The auxiliary-bearing constructor requires an explicit validated reader path. Snapshot address behavior, appearance assignment, selection, visibility, camera, textures, viewport and archived previews still need evidence for a full saved-source writer. The [scope and measurements](../../../../re/Fusion/SavedCuboidSnapshot/ReadbackBoundaries.md) give the exact boundaries.
