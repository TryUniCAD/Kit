# SOLIDWORKS saved active owner research

The offline commands trace the measured 12 mm P03 derivative's active
Parasolid partition using two kernel-verified layouts. They require the
exact saved file and the two descriptor receipts. The commands retain
source attributes and report unresolved records without skipping them.
[Usage and measured scope](../../../../re/solidworks/records/SavedActiveOwnerBoundary.md)

The exact walk reached the terminator after 189 records and resolved
all declared references. Independent checks verified both trim-to-line
owner chains and all measured boundary relationships. The existing
typed table and model builders reconstructed a closed 9,600 mm³ solid
with all twelve measured edge pairs and six outward face rings.

Three genuine vertices carry automation attributes that the general
scanner rejects. The research check retained and verified those
attributes on the original bytes. The two free trim records are
separate from the twelve line carriers consumed by the boundary.
Fresh offline processes reproduced the complete walk, vertex
verification and lower geometry. A different owner descriptor was
refused before output.

Public B-rep recovery, source annotation binding and whole-source
conversion are separate work. The research ran without a CAD process
or production SDK changes.
