# One native case can withhold an entire SOLIDWORKS batch

A finished capture phase only attests the process lifetime. Each case still
needs its own reopen, geometry, feature and archive checks.

Three thin-wall measurements failed in the paused 62-case catalogue.
The prior worker withheld every case in those three batches. Verification
of the preserved receipts found six companion cases that independently met
their native checks. The rejected thin walls extended into negative local V.

After both native phases finish, record a case-level verification refusal
and withhold its accepted bundle. The controller can still publish the
independently verified sibling bundles at their original roles.
