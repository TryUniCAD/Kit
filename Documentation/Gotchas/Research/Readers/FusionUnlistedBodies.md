# fusion retains body records outside the current collection

The Combine, CombineCut and CombineIntersect controls retain the second body's native record after the modeling operation. Its saved parent points at the bodies collection. The parent's current child list contains only the first body.

Counting every native body as current makes the independent vendor body count appear incorrect. Preserve each record for historical research and inspect both ends of collection membership. The Combine file has one listed body. Its volume and surface area match the vendor's one measured body. Its capture omits centroid.
