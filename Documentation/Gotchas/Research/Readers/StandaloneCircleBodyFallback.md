# A standalone circle must prove its empty body roster

The compatibility reader creates a fallback body record even when a P02 source
contains only a circular sketch. Counting sampled circles cannot distinguish that
case from an unresolved solid history. Requiring one exact saved empty-partition
fingerprint also rejects valid repeated saves and inch-display controls.

Require zero owned configuration atoms and regions and consume the complete
standalone sketch history through its footer. Bind diameter, ordered Origin
coincidence and support from their actual typed references. Refuse body removal
when a decoded BRep contains a solid. Extra authored history and contradictory
records must retain an unresolved compatibility result and refuse conversion.
Keep uninterpreted state and native/cache evidence explicit; these facts do not
release a native translation or pair qualification gate.
