# Placed Pad ordinals use composed locations

A single saved OCCT TShape can appear at two different positions in one Pad. Its lower and upper boundaries then receive separate indexed entities. Different wrapper paths can also compose to the same symbolic datum chain and legitimately share one indexed entity.

Use the raw shape identity and the composed symbolic location for each ordinal. Compare the evaluated placement separately. Equal matrices alone cannot authorize merging distinct source identities. In the disclosed Pad, six placed faces use five raw records, twelve placed edges use eight, and eight placed vertices use four.
