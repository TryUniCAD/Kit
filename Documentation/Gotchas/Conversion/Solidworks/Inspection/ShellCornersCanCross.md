# Four corners can form a crossed shell stock sketch

Four distinct corners with two X and two Y coordinates can still form
a crossed loop. Swapping the upper vertex coordinates preserves the
point and line identities while replacing two sides with diagonals.
Building the shell from those bounds would disagree with its editable
sketch.

Check that every actual line joins adjacent corners after checking the
closed incidence graph. The reader can derive rectangular bounds only
after both checks pass.
