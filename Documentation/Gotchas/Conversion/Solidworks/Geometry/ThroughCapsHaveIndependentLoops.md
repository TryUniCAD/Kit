# A rectangular through-opening has an independent inner loop on each cap

A through-opening can look like one crossed profile when both openings
share an XY footprint. Its lower and upper faces each own a separate
inner loop. The terminal fins connect those loops to the four interior
walls. The P04 2026 capture stores the inner loop first on both caps.
Its signed XY areas reverse between caps because their effective outward
normals oppose each other.

Preserve the saved order of each face's loop chain. Identify the exterior
through actual containment and direction, then prove the two interior
footprints and their connecting walls. A blind recess has an interior
floor and a single cap opening. [Evidence and model checks](../../../../../re/solidworks/records/PlanarThroughOpening2026.md)
