# Thin wall dimension endpoints are in model space

The initial thin revolve observer expected the spatial wall endpoints to equal
the sketch-local span. Front cases hide the distinction. On disclosed Top and
Right SOLIDWORKS 2026 cases, the real D3 distance dimension stores both
endpoints in model space while the two sketch lines remain local.

Place the local radial span at the axial midpoint through the actual principal
frame. The dimension basis uses signed radial U, signed support normal and
negative support V. Project the first world endpoint into that inverse basis
to make the frame translation. Inactive D4 has neither spatial endpoint nor
frame. Verify both native-saved and edited sources before relying on this rule.
