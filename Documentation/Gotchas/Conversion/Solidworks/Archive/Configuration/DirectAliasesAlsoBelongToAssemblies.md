# Direct aliases also belong to assembly roots

The current archive field that registers direct header aliases is used by both the ordinary part root and the saved assembly model that seeds its mate archive. Restricting the shared emitter to the ordinary part owner prevented the assembly Config and MatesList SDK replay on all eight pinned assemblies.

Use the two concrete version-19000 owner classes already recognized by the linked reader. Keep the top-level body-writer depth, primary map identity and distinct external-object alias checks. The shared ghost count action also retains its separately verified populated-row profile and validates its 28-bit count and signed four-bit flags.
