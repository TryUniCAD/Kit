# Disabled native equations can retain their expression text

A SOLIDWORKS relation can retain its serialized formula after the native
mode word changes. The cache-free part reader's text scan can still find
the complete target and dependency names in that archive.

For the scoped local width divided by two equation, verify the actual
relation and the enabled word before binding the boss depth expression.
When the physical check fails, keep the source identity and diagnostic
on permissive inspection and omit the unqualified expression. Strict
reading fails with the physical diagnostic. Perform this check for raw
inspection too because the absence of a saved B-rep has no bearing on the
dimension's activation.
