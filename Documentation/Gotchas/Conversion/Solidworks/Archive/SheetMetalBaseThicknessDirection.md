# Verify native base-flange thickness direction

The first SP04.1 capture at revision f3c6c2f2 stopped at the positive-Z body bounds check. The diagnostic retry at c8d2a454 passed both stock controls and measured `ThickenDir=false` on the default Front Plane with a 41 mm by 23 mm profile and 1.25 mm thickness. The only native sheet-metal body occupied `(0, 0, -1.25)` to `(41, 23, 0)` mm. Applying `IBaseFlangeFeatureData.ReverseThickness` through the typed feature definition and rebuilding left the same bounds despite a successful modification call.

Choose the thickness direction at creation via `InsertSheetMetalBaseFlange2`. The next capture uses its `ThickenDir=true` argument and still requires the exact positive-Z extreme-point bounds before saving. Keep the native bounds in the receipt.
