# Chamfer text clearance and catalog identity have independent owners

A chamfer's display can initially place D1 text at a clearance equal to its drive distance. In the disclosed axial chamfer, editing the actual distance from 4.125 to 5.625 mm leaves the clearance at 4.125 mm. The same annotation ID 201 belongs to `*Back` in that part and `*Left` in a second part. The value dimension frame follows the local catalog rotation and its origin follows the edge and D1.

Construct the frame from the actual registered annotation owner and the selected edge. Preserve the independently authored text location when refreshing witness geometry.
