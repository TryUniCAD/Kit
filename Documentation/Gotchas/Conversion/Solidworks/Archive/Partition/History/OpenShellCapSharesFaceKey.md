# Saved open-shell cap and rim share a face key

A saved rectangular open shell can keep the same `FACE_ID_2001` producer,
creation event and EndFace ordinal on both the original top cap and the
resulting rim. The original cap belongs to the preceding stock mark.
The active face with that key owns two trim loops around the opening.

Resolve the selected cap against the stock's actual rollback state.
The shared key cannot disambiguate the two physical faces across states.
