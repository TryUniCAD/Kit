# Full-ellipse readback and publication bind different archive layers

The calibrated cold reader can verify a regenerated full ellipse against all
ten clear streams. Native measurements bind the complete file, including its
encoded self locator and compression. Matching clear streams alone leaves the
full-file digest unverified. An otherwise identical unmeasured codec result or
new locator cannot inherit the original native acceptance flags.

For SDK output use the original measurement envelope, written epoch and
locator. Verify exact complete bytes before exposing them. The strict ellipse
reader can still project the typed cold body when its ten clear streams match
on another compression runtime. Record fresh native evidence before
publishing a different complete payload.
