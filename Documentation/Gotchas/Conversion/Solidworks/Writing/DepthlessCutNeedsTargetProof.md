# A depthless through cut needs proof from the target writer

## Expectation

A valid neutral through-all extrusion can omit its primary length because the target determines the intersection with the explicitly selected stock.

## Actual behavior

The shared native guard also sees this representation for writers that cannot reconstruct a depthless extrusion. It refused the two-stock SOLIDWORKS profile before the registry staged a destination or the direct adapter entered its emitter. The internal preparer could already generate the complete measured part.

## Resolution

Ask the selected writer to validate the actual neutral document and destination. The measured SOLIDWORKS planner proves one plain through-all cut on the root part. Only that feature can omit its primary length under the native guard. Other writers, nested documents, native-only definitions and other termination modes keep their original checks.
