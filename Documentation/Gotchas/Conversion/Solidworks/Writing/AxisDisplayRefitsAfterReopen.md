# The edited finite axis display refits on fresh reopen

A successful live rebuild changes the signed box stock. Trial02
observed all three selectable origin axes keeping their original
finite display intervals during the three independent driver edits.
The next fresh open of each saved part refitted only the interval
for the edited dimension. Both selected planes and the signed
through-origin line stayed valid during every stage.

The measurement gate checks the original finite display during live
edit and the recomputed interval after fresh reopen. The changing
span describes presentation, while the actual owned planes determine
the axis direction and carrier.
