# Sketch origin coincidence uses an external point

A fully constrained rectangle can retain an actual relation to the document origin even though all of its entities are in one part. The sketch records the origin as an external point referencing the separate origin profile. Its type-9 relation binds that point to the corner or the local center. A centered rectangle has two additional point-to-line relations for its construction diagonals.

Preserve the origin reference and distinguish its local point slot from its target feature and point. Check the affected corner or center after independent length edits. In the six disclosed siblings, the relation array carries these bindings and the separate coincidence maps are empty.
