# The final through-all body is not the selected stock atom

## Expectation

A portable part with one final body can bind that body's identity to the one cold atom created for its stock boss. Both refer to the only solid in the design.

## Actual behavior

The body is after the cut. The selected-stock atom is the output of the preceding boss and the input to the cut. The native selected-body wrapper retains that actual stock atom and its producing feature. Native cut regeneration creates a distinct result and chooser state. Relabeling the stock atom as the final body silently changes the producer relationship even when the eventual solid volume appears correct.

The source also exposes both stock and cut-profile semantic parents. The native cut history list contains only its own profile. The actual producer dependency travels through the body wrapper and atom graph. Adding the stock as another native history-list item is not equivalent.

## Workaround

Retain the portable final body identity in the complete original envelope. In a partial cold owner graph, represent its actual cut producer with an explicit pending-regeneration role. Generate the real selected-stock atom and wrapper through the shared manager/base-walk rules. Keep cut output, chooser and kernel caches absent until a proven regeneration path supplies them. Do not certify complete source conversion from the partial graph.

The [construction record](../../../../../../re/solidworks/records/ThroughAllSourceOwners2026.md) and [source-only receipt](../../../../../../re/solidworks/measurements/2026/ThroughAllSourceOwners01.json) bind the evidence. The separate [selected-body native proof](../../../../../../re/solidworks/measurements/2026/SdkThroughAllSelectedBodyNative01.json) preserves the measured consumed-stock/result distinction.
