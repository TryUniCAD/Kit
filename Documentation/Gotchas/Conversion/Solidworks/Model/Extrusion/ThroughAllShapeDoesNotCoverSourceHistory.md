## Expectation

An ordinary through-all source can be reduced to its stock and cut while preserving the part, because its body, volume and editable stock length still match.

The three disclosed sources have 28 ordinary timeline rows, 21 actual typed roots, three planes, three selections and a distinct circle-centre point. The strict constructor's smaller neutral shape does not cover these owners. The source presentation projector also reports the complete two-operation layout as unqualified.

Preserve explicit stock and axis ownership and the source's actual dimensions. Never promote a solid-only comparison to complete source acceptance.
