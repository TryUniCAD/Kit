# The cold two-stock part has implicit system children

## Expectation

A selected two-stock through cut needs the persisted cabinet and environment children required by the saved presentation profile.

## Actual behavior

The admitted initial graph has 23 actual roots and 23 public timeline rows. Its cabinet and environment collections contain no stored children. The ordinary saved presentation gate rejects the initial graph.

## Workaround

Require proven provisional stock ownership before admitting the empty collections. Validate the complete public root inventory and the absence of stored configuration helpers. Keep saved owner checks on the saved path. [Cold admission and measurements](../../../../../../re/solidworks/records/TwoStockColdSdkRead2026.md)
