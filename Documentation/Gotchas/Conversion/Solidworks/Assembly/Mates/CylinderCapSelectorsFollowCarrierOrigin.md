# A carrier origin can be at either cylinder cap

The ordinary D01 source cylinder and its independently generated counterpart have the same physical caps. The saved source uses its blind end as the kernel carrier origin with a negative local axis. The frozen generated child uses its sketch plane with a positive local axis. Their cap selectors agree in carrier-relative coordinates and exchange physical roles. Match the two cap faces through the actual sketch plane, blind depth and reciprocal circular rims.

The active mate wrapper owns the signed direction independently of the kernel axis. Its far extent can be negative when the direction points away from the other rim. Preserve the actual local and world wrapper directions and calculate the signed extent from the owned rim geometry.
