# Fusion appearance alias selection can depend on the caller mode

A saved body's material reference can name an exact base asset and have a separate `_Post2015` compatibility partner. The Fusion 2706.1.17 asset module has two inspected lookups that require the exact base. One lookup keeps the base when both exist. In the other lookup, caller mode `2` selects the partner when both exist.

Keep the two assets and their owner relationships when preserving stored state. Treat Kit's existing suffix preference as its own reader behavior until the actual body-open caller and mode are measured. A successful open of an intact pair shows the result for that document, while base-only, partner-only and caller-mode behavior require distinguishing evidence.
