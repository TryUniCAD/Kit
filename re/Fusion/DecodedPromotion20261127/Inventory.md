## Distinct production layers

`ReadDocument` and `EmitParsed` provide typed complete-document transport for their accepted schemas and geometry models. They do not require the strict neutral projector to recognize the source. The public CLI native decode layer exposes that distinction. A successful typed decode is not complete neutral recovery, source-free synthesis, or conversion acceptance.

The public strict route is `FusionReader.Read` → `ReadStrict` → `ProjectNative`. Auxiliary-bearing native sources enter `ProjectVendor`; it dispatches to bounded circular, prism or rectangular-cut projectors. The broader partial reader retains explicit limitations and is not a writer contract.

`MakeFusionDoc(DocumentSpec(...))` is source-free authoring through its declared specification. `FusionWriter` accepts only exact qualified `fusion.authoring` or `fusion.native_state` contracts. `EncodeDocument` regenerates and compares the complete authoring graph, or emits/decodes the typed native state and reprojects it for full neutral equality. A changed neutral field cannot override retained native state. A native-state reconstruction is same-document transport, not synthesis of an arbitrary neutral graph.
