## Qualified profile and evidence

The ordinary writer accepts two additional millimetre profiles: one closed analytic circle with a fixed centre and a driving diameter on the canonical Front/+Z plane with a forward blind boss; or a driving radius on canonical Top/+Y with a reversed blind boss. One independent active configuration, one support, one sketch, one creating extrusion and one body must satisfy the existing complete-document and actual-owner guards.

## Production boundary

SldprtWriter keeps the existing polygon planner first. Only a typed rejection permits PlanCircle, which applies the same complete-document/capability guards and the two explicit profile gates. PrepareNative preserves the original polygon construction path unchanged and routes other graphs through PrepareCircle. Both selection and direct emission therefore require the same circle ownership and end-condition proof.

The four new preparation modules reuse ProjectCircle, MakePartRecipe, FinalPartPath, BindPartIntent and MakeCirclePart. They introduce no saved fields, patches, field programs, payloads, CAD dependencies or file lookup. Every serialized field still comes from the existing model constructors and serializers. [Circle construction](SdkCircleBoss2026.md), [end owners](SdkCircleEndConditions2026.md) and [centre fixation](CircleCenterFixation2026.md) remain the field authorities.

Writer discovery remains part-only with the same six native capabilities. Its assembly/drawing aliases stay absent, and reader discovery is unchanged. Capability declarations describe transfers for a graph that passes supports; they do not enable other sketch constraints, property data or end-condition families.
