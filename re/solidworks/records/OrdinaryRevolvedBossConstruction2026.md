# Generated ordinary revolved boss candidate for SOLIDWORKS 2026

The research part builder constructs one ordinary solid revolve and its consumed sketch from neutral inputs. This witness targets a closed, axis-aligned rectangle to the right of a straight construction axis in principal-plane sketch coordinates. The axis points upward and has a model-space span of at least `1e-8 m`. The sweep is one-direction blind with `1e-8 < angle_rad <= 2π`, a separate Boolean reversal, and degree presentation.

## Input and graph

Call `construct_part` with the usual registry, provenance, presentation, target and optional native file identity. Supply `feature_kind="revolve"` in the document, with `revolution_name`, the four perimeter `vertices_m`, `axis_start_m`, `axis_end_m`, `angle_rad` and `reverse_direction`. The axis endpoints and perimeter vertices each contain two sketch-space coordinates in metres. Existing authored part fields supply the principal plane, document and configuration identity, and density. The builder exposes the revolve through `PartConstruction.revolution`. Its extrusion property stays empty so assembly-child bounds validation continues to require a forward blind extrusion. ## Vendor measurement

Use the pinned SOLIDWORKS 2026 SP05.2 oracle and a fresh process for each candidate. Verify its SHA-256 and place it at the explicit path authored into its file identity. `InspectGeneratedRevolves.py` accepts a pinned job linking the candidate and generator receipt digests and brackets the batch with healthy, separately launched controls. It calls `AuthorRevolveSources.run_phase(model, "reopen", path, oracle_sha256, sha256)` with the generator receipt's `com_request` as `model`. The run confirms the consumed sketch and selected line, exact direction and positive angle, zero errors and warnings, one body, analytic volume, area and centre, and a successful in-memory driving-angle edit. It checks the original digest after each probe. Open/rebuild and in-memory editing have separate results, especially for the full turn. A saved edit and a fresh reopen require a subsequent phase. [Diagnostic inspector](../../tooling/sw2026/InspectGeneratedRevolves.py).

The first controlled open returned warning `32` for each candidate, and subsequent forced rebuilds failed. With the selected sketch's creation stamp corrected, all three discovery files opened cleanly, rebuilt the expected geometry, and passed driving-angle edits. The saved-edit reopen and two sealed held-out cases remain pending. Optional visual states, additional features, assembly-child bounds for revolves and user-facing writer enablement are outside the change.
