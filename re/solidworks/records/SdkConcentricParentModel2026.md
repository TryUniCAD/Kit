## Construction

`MakeCylAsm` accepts the actual generated `PartCandidate`, its allocated
face tag, ordinary assembly recipe and two proper rigid occurrences.
`CylMateIn` supplies the actual ordered selection role, alignment and
rotation lock, and optionally the two checked child-local axes. The
fixed index is relative to the authored endpoint order. The shared
occurrence, configuration, header and VisualStates constructors retain
their existing inputs and allocation.

The constructor checks five load-critical linked child streams against
the actual in-memory typed graph. It parses only that newly generated
child's own in-memory partition and delta history. It binds the lateral
face to the live extrusion, sketch arc and fixed centre, and checks its
radius or diameter driver and the actual body atom. Two lateral loops
supply the cap selectors and rim centres. The allocated face tag must
be an exact positive 16-bit match for its generated `SWEntUnchanged`
word in this scoped profile.

The active wrapper stores the true occurrence file and configuration,
two distinct placement snapshots and the signed local axis. Its world
axis and far extent follow each actual rigid pose and the two physical
rims. The detached wrapper independently belongs to the real parent
self component, with a distinct support cylinder and faceted node. It
uses the common neutral surface bounds and no orientation vector.
The active references share the same generated child feature file,
with their true cap/null order. See [face identities](ConcentricFaceIdentity2026.md),
[constructor bounds](ConcentricFaceTagAndExtent2026.md) and
[signed fields](ConcentricSignedWrapperConstruction2026.md).
