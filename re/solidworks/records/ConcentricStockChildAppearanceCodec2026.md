## The populated Definition owner

An ordinary version-19000 part's `uiPartDoc_c.has_pwmaterial=1` enables `f1110`, the embedded `PWMaterialManager_c` followed by its `suObList`. Each saved list contains two schema-one **`PWMaterialProperties_c`** objects.
