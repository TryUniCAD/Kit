## Independent material tree

Every assigned save carries `swXmlContents/MATERIALTREE`. The stream is BOM-marked UTF-16 XML with a `materials` root and distinct `curves`, `classification` and `configurationslist` branches. It carries physical properties, configuration association, shading, swatch, texture, hatch and other material-specific records. The steel material additionally uses its material model and curve data. Changing the depth preserves this stream byte for byte within each design after the material swap. The aluminum tree differs across the two designs even though the name, catalog ID, density and length agree. Its exact changed fields and their owners remain a separate correlation task. The unassigned control has no material tree. No XML stream is emitted or used as a model input in this research probe.

Eight fresh guarded processes read six saved source states, one prior diagnostic and the pristine control. All 24 complete `CMgr`, `Config-0` and linked `ResolvedFeatures` streams re-emit byte for byte. The readers derive the same typed owners without runtime file reads. Standalone XML structure and hashes are captured separately. The codec remains in research. Texture property semantics, renderer state, material tree production and optionality, arbitrary catalog identities and independent writer acceptance remain open.
