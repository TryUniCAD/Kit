## Context and inherited dispatch

The actual outer drawing condition is zero. The full view serializer `[0x4c958dc0,0x4c95d339)` sets `0x40` at `0x4c958f87` and restores it at `0x4c95cb24`. ## Reached dependency framing

The numeric record specifies the full order of each private dependency layout. `moCompNote_c` adds no fields to `moCompDetailItem_c`. The latter serializes its existing component-reference prefix, a local detail tag or external name pair selected by its **actual** component/filed-owner relationship, then the second detail tag and `f100:i32`. `moAttachPointData_c` owns two three-double points, a view tag and two doubles. The abstract `moAttachPoint_c` base owns two points, type word, view tag, two further points and one byte.
