## Neutral orientation and live edit semantics

The selected upward Front-plane line has ordered direction `+Y`. Positive right-hand rotation sends a radial `+X` point toward `−Z`, since `+Y × +X = −Z`. Native forward (`ReverseDirection = false`, `f824 = 0`) has negative-Z centroid for partial turns; reverse has positive-Z centroid. The selected endpoints and positive parameter stay unchanged. The old observer's `forward_handedness = -1` uses its own positive-Z helper convention; it must not be interpreted as a negative right-hand sweep.

The prototype emits an existing neutral `AngularRevolve` with the real positive angular parameter and actual `Selection` path to its construction entity. Forward uses the ordered unit axis; reverse negates only its effective direction. Source endpoint order, raw axis points and `f824` remain explicit provenance/attributes. The construction line itself is unchanged. Existing legacy `BuildRevolve` derives its `reversed` flag from boss/cut operation kind; that inference cannot supply this separate direction field.

The independent predictor derives inner/outer radii and axial limits from the actual contour and selected axis, then uses `V = h θ (ro² − ri²)/2` and the exact annular-sector surface/centroid integrals. All six recorded original native states, including the full-turn source's partial edited state, match. The three unchanged-source workers compare 30 distinct numeric fields; the presentation variant repeats ten comparisons, giving 40 visits. No generator or target-application acceptance is inferred from these reader checks.
