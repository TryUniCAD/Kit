# Model-only open-shell construction

The constructor projects a neutral rectangular Front Plane stock and one
inward shell. The selection is the stock's `face:1` end, and the wall
thickness has its own driving `D1`. The stock depth keeps its independent
driver. The stock shape and generated attachment history use the ordinary
stock allocator and body ownership rules.

The selected cap uses the actual four generated segment IDs. The
auxiliary edge uses the same consumed stock atom. The two dimension
witnesses are on the minimum-x side at the y midpoint, separated along
+x by the thickness. The display point follows the center of the cap.
The annotation frame is oriented along +x, −z and +y. The two disclosed
native saves corroborate the witness rule after an actual wall edit.

Three neutral requests constructed the complete shell feature node with
zero data-file reads or process launches. The translated requests moved
the display and witness points with the stock. Three requests were
refused before construction because their cap, wall direction or
cavity was outside the modeled profile.

the same baseline convention as the measured chamfer. In separate
processes, the three requests regenerated the same whole files and ten
streams per file. The types, cap, atom and wall dimension decode from
the written container. The linked Config-0 and ResolvedFeatures then
re-emit identically, with no unread suffix. The common archive allocator
hands off at map 91 and ends at 255. The translated and distinct requests
