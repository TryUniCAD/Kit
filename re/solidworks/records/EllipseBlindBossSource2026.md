# Ordinary full ellipse and blind boss source frontier

This B04-SW2026 PR #121 tranche covers one free, full, nonconstruction ellipse on the canonical origin Front Plane and one positive blind boss. The source protocol preserves the centre and signed major and minor construction points, the closed contour, the sketch support and the live D1-depth owner. Ellipse arcs, construction members, profile dimensions and relations, other support frames and other end modes are outside this calibration. Native acceptance and public adapter coverage remain open.

2. Rebuild cleanly. Require one configuration, one solid, zero sheet bodies, two outward planar caps and one lateral face, all owned by the actual boss.
