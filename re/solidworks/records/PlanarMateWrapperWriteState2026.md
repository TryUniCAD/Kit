# Planar mate wrapper and factory state in SOLIDWORKS 2026

This record covers one ordinary resolved two-plane coincident, distance or parallel mate in a single-configuration assembly of ordinary parts. It traces the concrete constructors, the plane-cache and ownership updates, the detached-reference map, and the independent fixed and solve states of each occurrence. Addresses refer to the preferred image of `sldmodu.dll`, SHA-256 `569a081928ad24afac5408cc15a1034d18e89b2c5001428a64115a3cc497067e`, at `0x4b140000`, from SOLIDWORKS 2026 SP05.2 build `34.5.2.0604`. The instruction boundaries, whole native function hashes, exception-directory fragments, RTTI bindings and imports are recorded in [native evidence](PlanarMateWrapperNative2026.json).

The existing source lock admits A17, A18 and A19 and their synchronized child parts. Their `Contents/Definition/MatesList` values were read through the typed graph and its actual resolved map. The previous archive check covers 51 logical streams. The present field observations are from those three disclosed mates. They do not establish generated mate acceptance. ### Fixed occurrence

The ordinary owner-taking `moReference_c` constructor writes `f400=3` at `0x4bbb7d0c` and independently initializes `f408=0` at `0x4bbb7d17`. An explicitly fixed neutral occurrence derives `f408=1` from the requested anchor. `moReference_c::fix` applies that flag at `0x4bbbe0f8` or `0x4bbbe108`. Its registered-assembly branch can notify assemblers and their live solvers. The fixed flag does not encode the solve result. `setSolveStatus` at `0x4bbcfca0` writes a separate caller-supplied status at `+0x400`, with a forced state `1` when `+0x538` holds a pointer. `moReference_c::Serialize` writes both as independent `u16` at `0x4b66f418` and `0x4b66f428`. The modern loader stores the raw status and normalizes the subsequent fixed word to a Boolean at `0x4b670028` and `0x4b670048`.

An occurrence generated before running the vendor solver can represent the anchor as `(f400, f408)=(3,1)`, while its floating peer is `(3,0)`. A17–A19 each save a fixed `(4,1)` and floating `(3,0)` after native solving. The exact solver transition to status `4` and the effect of opening a complete generated mate with the constructor status require independent verification. A cold serializer has no reason to copy that solved status from a saved assembly.
